FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my photos ready before we start?
You need the work selected, not edited for web. Choosing what goes in is the part only you can do, and it is usually what delays a build. We handle sizing, compression and delivery.
Can I update the galleries myself afterwards?
Yes — that is why we build on Webflow rather than something custom. Signature and Studio include CMS training. If you would rather not touch it, the care plan covers two gallery updates a month.
I already have a site. Is that a problem?
No, and it is often the better starting point — we can see what is already converting. Start with the 150 ₾ audit. Sometimes the honest answer is that your site is fine and your follow-up is what is broken.
Can you build me a print shop?
Yes, in the Studio package — but let's be straight about it: Webflow's commerce is thin for a serious print operation. If you plan to sell prints at volume we will recommend a dedicated commerce layer and tell you what that adds to the cost before you commit.
How do payments work?
Everything in GEL through Payze — cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Launch and Signature are 50% to start and 50% on launch. Studio is 40/30/30. Invoices are issued for each stage.
Do you work with photographers outside Georgia?
Yes, remotely — every site ships bilingual anyway. Calls run on Asia/Tbilisi time (UTC+4), so tell us your timezone and we will find a sane hour.
What if I only need it fast and cheap?
Then we are the wrong studio, and we would rather say so now than take your deposit. A template on Tilda or Wix will cost you a fraction of Launch. Come back when losing inquiries costs more than the site does.
